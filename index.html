About Us

About ALRANQQY

ALRANQQY is a glass and aluminium company based at Mai Tower, 1502, Al Nahda 1, Dubai, UAE. Our work is glass and aluminium installation and maintenance for homes, offices, shops and commercial buildings.

From a single sliding window to a complete shop front or office partition, we measure on site, fabricate to the exact size, install cleanly, and stay available afterwards for maintenance and repairs — rollers, locks, handles, hinges, sealing and glass replacement.

  • Aluminium & glass installation — new work, done to size
  • Maintenance & repair — existing windows, doors and glass
  • Free site visit and clear, item-wise quotation
  • Service across Dubai for homes, offices and shops
